# Debetter

Web app for organizing and running debate tournaments: organizers create tournaments, generate pairings round by round, and record results.

## Language

**Room**:
The physical space (e.g. "204") where a match takes place during a round. Assigned by the organizer after pairings exist.
_Avoid_: location, venue

**Pairing**:
The assignment of teams to a match within a round. Pairings are published to participants as a batch, not one by one.
_Avoid_: matchup, draw
